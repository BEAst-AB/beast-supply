
[[price]]
= Prices

All requested prices in the format are related to the article or service within this Pre-Award Catalogue Request. The following prices can be stated:

* Item price is the requested net price including all discounts and charges but excluded Vat
* Requested conditional price related to a specific location or a certain quantity

Be aware that no Requested Gross prices can be sent in the format (price before discount and charges). All prices shall have Currency as an attribute. 
Currency shall be according to Code list.
